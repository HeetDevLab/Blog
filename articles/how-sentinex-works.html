How Sentinex App Lock Works

HeetDevLabIndependent developer notes
Sentinex is designed as a lightweight Android app lock focused on privacy and performance. It avoids unnecessary tracking and keeps its core protection flow on the device.

1. App Usage Access Based Monitoring

Sentinex uses Android's app usage access to detect when protected apps are opened. When a protected app launch is detected, Sentinex presents its lock screen so the user can authenticate before continuing.

2. On-device Processing

The app's protection flow is designed to run locally on the device. Sentinex does not need a user account to perform its core app-lock function.

3. Lightweight Architecture

The app is designed to keep its background work focused on the app-lock experience and avoid unnecessary services.

Why Privacy-First Matters

Security tools handle sensitive parts of a person's digital life. Clear permissions, transparent behaviour and keeping control with the user are important parts of a trustworthy app.
